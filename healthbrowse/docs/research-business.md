# HealthBrowse research and commercialization plan

## Intended use and first customer

The first sellable workflow is an evidence-navigation workspace for medical educators and university research teams. A user investigates a public condition, inspects seven sections, follows a citation, examines a registered trial or funding award, and bookmarks the record for teaching or research. Version the evidence sources and preserve a reproducible audit trail for the study. A separate prospective review is needed before accepting identifiable patient data or offering patient-specific decisions. Record the intended audience, claims, testing evidence, jurisdiction and data flows with a qualified regulatory reviewer before making that change. [FDA clinical decision support guidance](https://www.fda.gov/regulatory-information/search-fda-guidance-documents/clinical-decision-support-software)

## Research protocol

**Primary question.** Does source-backed, seven-section navigation reduce time to a correctly sourced answer compared with ordinary search? **Secondary question.** Does field-level verification reduce unsupported answers relative to the same retrieval pipeline without its verification step? Preregister the task definitions, analysis plan, exclusions and success criteria before exposing participants.

| Study | Comparison and method | Outcomes |
|---|---|---|
| Offline retrieval | Freeze the corpus, retrieval code, vocabulary and ranking revision. Compare exact matching, phrase/alias matching and ranked search on the same prompts. Include misspellings, subtypes, multi-condition questions and unknown topics. | Top-five graded relevance (nDCG@5), proportion of answerable tasks retrieved, incorrect scope switches, latency and cost per query. |
| Evidence verification | For a stratified sample of source-linked claims, two independent reviewers label source identity, entailment, date, population and harm. Include unsupported, withdrawn and wrong-subtype examples. Compare full and verification-disabled outputs inside an isolated evaluation environment. | Unsupported clinical claims / reviewed claims; harmful supported-looking errors / all tasks; correct abstentions / unsupported tasks; answerable-query abstention rate. Report confidence intervals, disagreement and adjudication decisions. |
| Human usability | In a counterbalanced crossover, participants complete matched task sets on ordinary search and HealthBrowse. Randomize order. Use equivalent difficulty and different questions across conditions to reduce learning carryover. Obtain institutional approval for the participant protocol and consent before recruitment. | Time to a correct sourced answer, task success, citation inspection rate, confidence calibration and accessibility barriers. Analyze paired differences and report missing answers. |

Stratify by use case, focus area, reading level and disease subtype. Keep paraphrases, near-duplicate passages and questions derived from the same source family together when creating train/test splits. Include topics beyond the initial 20. Keep the evaluation labels out of retriever/ranking development. Use an independent adjudicator for reviewer disagreements. Define adequate sample size from pilot variance and a prespecified meaningful effect with a statistician. Archive the task set version, code commit, index hash, retrieval date, prompts, filters and reviewer instructions. Keep research logs free of identifiers.

Run `node scripts/maintenance_eval.mjs` for a small reproducible term-resolution smoke experiment. It prints exact-search and phrase-routing results, denominators, per-query outcomes and the input hash. It does not establish clinical accuracy, population representativeness, or a benefit over general-purpose web search. The existing program tests independently cover important software invariants; reviewer-labeled data and prospective study results remain to be collected.

**Proposed release gate.** Every displayed clinical claim in the audit sample must have an inspectable source; any adjudicated severe harmful claim triggers a release hold. For the usability study, define the target improvement and non-inferiority margin for task accuracy before collection. Small samples with zero observed failures still require confidence intervals. Do not advertise a hallucination-free or clinically certified product from these results.

## Institution pilot and business model

| Stage | Owner and concrete deliverable | Gate |
|---|---|---|
| Discovery | Product lead interviews 5–10 educators, medical librarians and research leads about how they currently verify a citation. Collect workflow timings, buying owner and procurement constraints. | At least two independent teams describe the same recurrent task and agree to test a prototype. |
| Scoped pilot | Faculty sponsor and product lead agree on public-topic-only use, named reviewers, permitted sources, support hours, data deletion and an incident contact. Include opt-in telemetry and an exit path. | Signed pilot scope and defined evaluation tasks. Review data protection and licensing with the institution. |
| Pilot review | Compare task success and time against baseline. Review source gaps, safety-critical incidents, active users and repeat use. | Predefined clinical, privacy and usability criteria met; reviewer and institution sign off on the pilot scope. |
| Paid research workspace | Offer institution seats or a semester pilot with saved research, curated reading lists, source provenance and agreed service/support terms. Measure willingness to pay in interviews. | A signed paid agreement, acceptable gross margin and successful restore/rollback exercise. Paid pricing, contracted institutions and revenue are not established here. |

Track cost per successfully completed research task and monthly gross margin. Let (M) be annual contract revenue per institution, (F) annual fixed operating expense and (V) annual variable expense per institution. Approximate break-even institutions as `ceil(F / (M - V))` when `M > V`. Include cloud, email, data/terminology licensing, clinical review, support, security and audit costs. Do not assume a free infrastructure allocation covers staff or licensed clinical content. Keep sponsorship and paid placement out of evidence ranking.

Feedback sentiment can route frustrated users to support and positive reports to usability follow-up. Evidence disputes require the reported claim, source citation, revision and qualified review. Polarity and frequency alone cannot establish truth. Track time to acknowledge, time to resolve, reopened issues and the number of corrections that reach users.

## Lightweight iOS and Android

Start with one responsive HTTPS web application. Support a home-screen icon, app manifest, standalone launch and a small service worker when available. Keep search, citations, study records and account operations functional through the same API as desktop. Cache only a versioned public shell; do not cache authenticated API responses, chat history, contact forms or research records that may be stale. For offline use, provide a connection message and a return path. Test mobile Safari and Chrome, portrait and landscape orientation, large fonts, screen readers and keyboard navigation on target devices. [Apple home-screen web apps](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/open-as-web-app-iphea86e5236/ios) · [web.dev PWA guide](https://web.dev/learn/pwa/)

If store distribution is required, plan Android as an Android App Bundle with a Trusted Web Activity backed by a verified domain and Digital Asset Links, or use a native shell with genuine platform features. On iOS, test a suitable native wrapper only after defining features such as citation sharing and accessible saved reading lists that meet App Review expectations. Plan for developer enrolment, signing, privacy declarations, account deletion, healthcare-content review, sandbox testing and store approvals. Store builds are separate release artifacts. [Android Trusted Web Activities](https://developer.chrome.com/docs/android/trusted-web-activity/) · [Apple App Review Guidelines](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/) · [Apple Developer Program enrolment](https://developer.apple.com/help/account/membership/program-enrollment/)
